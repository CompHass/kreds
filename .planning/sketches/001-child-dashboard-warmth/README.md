# Sketch 001: Child Dashboard Warmth

## Design Question

How can the child dashboard keep the Stitch energy while feeling more warm, age-appropriate, and clear?

## How to View

open .planning/sketches/001-child-dashboard-warmth/index.html

## Variants

- **A: Mission Board** — Keeps the game-like quest board but softens copy and prioritizes the next faithful action.
- **B: Stewardship Garden** — Uses growth metaphors, calmer cards, and warm progress language. Winner.
- **C: Wallet Constellation** — Keeps the neon/futuristic feel while organizing money into clear stewardship planets.

## Selected Direction

Variant B keeps the energy of Stitch but makes the child experience more age-appropriate through growth language, softer hierarchy, and a calmer metaphor for stewardship. This is the best baseline for children ages 6+ because it makes responsibility feel cultivated rather than game-punitive.

## What to Look For

Compare which variant feels most child-friendly without losing Kreds' distinct visual energy. Pay attention to whether firstfruits, tasks, wishlist, and giving are easy to understand at a glance.
