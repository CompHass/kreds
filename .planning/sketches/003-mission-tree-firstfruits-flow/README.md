# Sketch 003: Mission Tree Firstfruits Flow

## Design Question

How should a completed mission become earnings, firstfruits, generosity, savings, and gratitude without feeling transactional or punitive?

## How to View

open .planning/sketches/003-mission-tree-firstfruits-flow/index.html

## Variants

- **A: Storybook Flow** — A guided narrative from mission seed to golden basket.
- **B: Ledger Ladder** — A clearer financial breakdown for older children and parent trust.
- **C: Tree Growth Ceremony** — A celebratory moment where completion grows the tree and opens gratitude. **Winner.**

## Decision

Use **C: Tree Growth Ceremony** for the child-facing completion moment. Pair it with audit details from B in parent/history views so the celebration stays joyful without hiding ledger integrity.

## What to Look For

Choose the flow that makes firstfruits automatic and understandable while preserving joy, agency, and auditability.
