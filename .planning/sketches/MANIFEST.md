# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

Use the existing Stitch `Vibrant Neo-Glass` direction as the base, but soften it for children ages 6+ and Christian family stewardship. Preserve the dark glass, neon accents, Montserrat/Inter/JetBrains Mono typographic contrast, pill actions, and tactile neo-brutalist shadows, while reducing cyberpunk aggression, improving trust, and making gratitude/generosity feel warm rather than transactional.

## Key Decisions

- Child-facing screens should soften Stitch's cyberpunk edge with growth/care metaphors while keeping dark glass and neon accents.
- Parent-facing management should use a command-center layout with explicit approval queue and audit rail.
- Generosity and gratitude should be framed as a guided blessing path: responsibility, firstfruits, generosity, gratitude.
- Kreds do Bem copy must continue to avoid real-money donation implications in v1.
