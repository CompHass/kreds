# Sketch 003: Generosity Gratitude Tone

## Design Question

How should Kreds do Bem and weekly gratitude feel generous and biblical without becoming visually noisy or guilt-driven?

## How to View

open .planning/sketches/003-generosity-gratitude-tone/index.html

## Variants

- **A: Giving Moment** — A focused Kreds do Bem flow with warm matching explanation and internal-allocation language.
- **B: Gratitude Report** — Weekly snapshot with scripture reference, reflection prompt, and family conversation tone.
- **C: Blessing Path** — Combines giving and gratitude as a guided child-safe story arc. Winner.

## Selected Direction

Variant C best connects responsibility, firstfruits, generosity, and gratitude into a child-safe story arc. It avoids guilt-driven donation language and frames giving as a supervised family practice.

## What to Look For

Compare which variant feels most faithful, warm, and non-coercive. Watch whether the UI avoids donation/tax/payment implications while still making generosity motivating.
