# Sketch 002: Parent Trust Console

## Design Question

How should the parent/family management surface balance neon personality with financial trust and auditability?

## How to View

open .planning/sketches/002-parent-trust-console/index.html

## Variants

- **A: Command Center** — Desktop-first layout with child sidebar, approval queue, and audit rail. Winner.
- **B: Ledger First** — Puts explainable financial history at the center to maximize trust.
- **C: Family Rhythm** — Organizes the week around family cadence, pending actions, and stewardship conversations.

## Selected Direction

Variant A gives parents the clearest operational control without abandoning the Kreds personality. The child sidebar, approval queue, and audit rail make the trust model visible and should guide parent-facing desktop layouts.

## What to Look For

Compare which variant makes parents feel most in control while still feeling like Kreds. Watch whether auditability, approvals, and child context are visible without overwhelming the screen.
