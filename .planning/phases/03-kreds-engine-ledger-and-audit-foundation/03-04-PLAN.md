    postReversal(command: ReversalCommand):
    - Antes de inserir: buscar a transação original pelo command.correctsTransactionId no DB; verificar que ledgerTransactions.familyId === command.familyId — se não bater, lançar Error('cross_family_reversal_forbidden') (Pitfall 3)
    - Buscar todas as ledgerLines da transação original para obter os amounts originais
    - Dentro de db.transaction: insert em ledgerTransactions com transactionType='reversal', correctsTransactionId=command.correctsTransactionId, note=command.correctionNote, commandId=command.commandId
    - Para cada linha original: insert em ledgerLines com amount = -(linha_original.amount) (contrapartida negativa), mesmo accountType da linha original, childProfileId do command, transactionId do novo header
    - Linhas originais NÃO são tocadas (LEDG-01, LEDG-08)
    - Retorna o txHeader

    getGuardianLedgerHistory(childProfileId: string, familyId: string):
    - SELECT: transactionId, commandId, transactionType, note, correctsTransactionId, createdAt (todos do header), lineId, accountType, amount (das lines)
    - FROM ledgerTransactions LEFT JOIN ledgerLines ON transactionId
    - WHERE child_profile_id = childProfileId AND family_id = familyId
    - ORDER BY createdAt DESC
    - Inclui commandId e note (D-09)

    getChildLedgerHistory(childProfileId: string, familyId: string):
    - SELECT: transactionId, transactionType, createdAt (header), accountType, amount (lines)
    - OMITE commandId, note, correctsTransactionId (D-10)
    - Mesmo FROM/JOIN/WHERE/ORDER que guardian
    - A label de exibição ('reversal' → 'Correção aplicada') é aplicada na UI, não na query
  </behavior>
