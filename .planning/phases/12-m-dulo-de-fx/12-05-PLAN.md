    - Dependency array: `[]` (somente ao montar)

    - Para cada PO: `const brl = getBrlAmount(po.foreignAmount, po.diRate)`, `const cv = getCurrentValue(po.foreignAmount, effectiveRate(po))`, `const res = getResult(brl, cv)`, `const pct = getResultPct(res, brl)`
